The app

Colette is an iOS app for composing carousel posts — photos, text, stickers, frames, shapes, drawings and video arranged across slides, then exported to your photo library. Editing and export run on your device. Colette has no accounts and does not host, publish or distribute anything you create; posting your exported slides anywhere is your own action, subject to that platform's rules.

Your content

Everything you import and create stays yours. Colette claims no rights over your photos, videos or finished carousels. You are responsible for holding the rights to material you import, and for how you use what you export.

Subscription

Colette is free to download and free to use for composing and exporting carousels as images. An optional subscription — Colette Pro — unlocks additional capabilities:

  • Video layers on slides
  • Slideshow video export
  • Lasso erase in background tools
  • Premium frames
  • Projects longer than 9 slides (up to 20)

Billing, renewal and cancellation

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple through your Apple Account, not by us. Payment is charged at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically at the end of each period unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before that period ends; Apple charges the renewal within 24 hours before the current period ends.

Cancelling stops future renewals; the current period runs to its end. Refunds are handled by Apple under its own policy — we cannot issue them.

If you reinstall the app or use another device signed into the same Apple Account, use Settings → Subscription → Restore Purchases to re-activate your subscription.
